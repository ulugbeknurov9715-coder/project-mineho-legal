語辞漢読

Privacy Policy and Terms of Service

Last updated: May 30, 2026

語辞漢読 is a Japanese learning dictionary app by Project 峯穂. It is designed to help users search words, save study history, and export study PDFs.

Privacy Policy

Information Stored on Your Device

語辞漢読 stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Search history
  • Study session words
  • PDF export history
  • PDF export snapshot data
  • Cached dictionary results
  • App settings such as theme, language, and PDF export preferences

This data is used to provide app features such as history review, study sessions, PDF export, PDF re-export, and faster dictionary access.

Online Dictionary Lookup

When you search for a word, the app may send the searched word to an online dictionary or API service in order to retrieve dictionary results.

The app may save dictionary results locally so it can build a local word library over time.

Personal Data

語辞漢読 does not require you to create an account.

語辞漢読 does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information.

語辞漢読 does not sell personal data.

PDF Files

PDF files are generated from your search history or study session data. The generated PDF files are saved or shared only when you choose to export them.

Clearing Data

The app allows you to clear search history and PDF history by selected time ranges. Clearing search history does not delete cached dictionary data. Clearing PDF history does not delete search history.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party dictionary or API services to provide search results. These services may process the searched word in order to return dictionary data.
